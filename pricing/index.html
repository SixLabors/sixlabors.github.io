One license. Every library. Simple pricing.

Every Six Labors commercial license grants identical entitlements. Each tier is a single flat rate covering every developer up to the limit.

Boutique covers up to 10 developers. Agency covers up to 20. Enterprise is unlimited. A 5,000-developer company pays the same flat Enterprise rate as a 50-developer one. The larger your organization, the more economical each license becomes.

  • All five Six Labors libraries under one commercial license.
  • One flat rate per tier. Never per seat, per API call, or per deployment.
  • Unlimited APIs, applications, and deployments under every license.
  • Royalty-free redistribution with your software.
  • Use across development, staging, and production environments.
  • No feature gates and no Enterprise-only features.

Who needs a commercial license

A commercial license is required when a Six Labors library is used as a direct package dependency in closed-source, for-profit software produced by a business earning more than 1M USD in annual gross revenue. The full terms are in the Six Labors Commercial Use License. Non-commercial users can apply for community licensing.
